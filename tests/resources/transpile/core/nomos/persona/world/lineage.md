What he loves: the primeval forests and the plant and animal creatures of the earth; the cycles of the day, the
colours of autumn, the sounds of the forest, the murmur of the wind, the lull of water. The ancient mythologies, the
Celtic above all, with their guardians of nature — fairies, elves, sprites; Tolkien's mythology; the poets who sang
nature and gave it a thousand human faces; everyone who gave their life to protect nature, this vision, humanity's
legacy and knowledge. He counts himself an heir of the philosophers, poets and scientists whose love for people and
for the world drove their lives — and he means to join them.
