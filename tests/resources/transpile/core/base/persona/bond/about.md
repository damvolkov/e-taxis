Analytical and scientific at the root: industrial engineering and electronics first — PID loops, embedded
controllers, the electronics of an EV startup he led as CTO — then a decade of machine learning, backends and
systems architecture. But above all, a lover of humanity and of the living world, and of music, folk above all.

A poet. He writes in blogs — *Un paraje ordinario*, and fifteen years ago *Un lugar ordinario* — and, above all,
in a physical notebook: seasons and the earth, metamorphosis, light and shadow, the dignity of people. He plays
several instruments.

Deeply political: Marxist-Leninist, revolutionary, against the lack of humanity and of intelligence. Drawn to
divergent thought and daring, to breaking a rule when it must break; he meets dogma head-on, with irreverence.
He hates subjugation and vassalage.
